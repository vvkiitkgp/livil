What Livil is

Livil is a social app made just for music. Repost the songs that say how you feel, start conversations with a track, and see what your friends are listening to. Independent artists upload their music here for people who actually love to listen, and see honestly how it lands.

How it works

Music says what words can't.

Your mood, as a song

Repost the part of a song that matches how you feel today, with a line about why. Your friends get it. You get theirs.

Start the conversation with a song

Send a track instead of a “hey”. They play it right inside the chat, and send one back.

Know what your friends are into

See who's listening right now, and to what. One tap and you're hearing the same song.

Explore

Your next favourite song, from a friend.

Every repost is someone you know saying “this one”. Follow the people whose taste you trust, star the artists you love, and new music finds you.

What's inside

Everything you need to share a song.

Livil is the social app made for music. Share how you feel with a song, talk in songs, and discover independent artists through the people you know.

Repost your mood

Clip your favourite part of a song, say how it makes you feel, and repost it to your profile.

Songs in chat

Send a song in any chat. Friends play it right there, react, and reply with one of their own.

Listening now

See which friends are playing music right now, and play the same song with one tap.

Know how it lands

Artists see plays, likes, reposts and comments on every track. Honest signals from real listeners.

Friends first

A feed of what your friends listen to, not an algorithm. Show love by liking and commenting on their posts. Their public playlists live on their profile.

Star artists

Star the artists you love. Every new upload shows up in your feed. No subscriptions, no bell icon.

Upload your music

Post your own tracks and credit everyone on them. Tag your guitarist, your drummer, your producer.

Playlists and albums

Build them alone or with friends. Public or private, your call.

For independent artists

Made music? Get it heard by people who love to listen.

Livil is a social app built only for music, so the people here came to listen. Upload your songs and find out, honestly, how they land.

  • Upload audio or video, from your phone or your browser.
  • Credit everyone on it: your guitarist, your drummer, your producer.
  • See plays, likes, reposts and comments on every track.
  • Read what listeners say, and reply to them.
