Somewhere along the way, listening got lonely.

We made mixtapes. We traded CDs. We sat in a room and pressed play together.

Then music moved into a feed.

A hundred million songs — and somehow we listen to them alone.

The algorithm knows what you'll play next. It has no idea who you'd play it with.

So we're building the other thing.

Music you feel with people. Not a feed you scroll alone.

01 — WHAT WE BELIEVE

People, not algorithms.

The best song you'll hear this year comes from a person — a friend, a stranger with taste — not a recommendation engine guessing at you.

02

A room, not a feed.

Listening together should feel like being in the same room. So that's what we made — rooms where the music stays in sync and the conversation lives beside it.

03

Presence, not posts.

See what your friends are playing, right now. Music feels different when you know someone else is in it with you.

04

Made together.

Playlists, rooms, moments — built by people, with people. Every part of Livil gets better the more of you are in it.
