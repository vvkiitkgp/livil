How is the Livil music score calculated?

The number on your Livil profile. It grows as you use Livil.

  • Listen to a song+1
  • Like a post+1
  • Comment+2
  • Share a song in chat+2
  • Repost a song+5
  • Host a Jam+5
  • Upload your own song+25
  • Someone plays your upload+1
  • Someone likes your repost+1
  • Someone comments on your repost+2

Updates every night. Daily limits keep it fair.

Daily limits
  • Listening: one point per song a day, up to 50 a day (Livil songs you play, or Spotify songs you open).
  • Comments: up to 20 a day. Songs shared in chat: up to 10 a day.
  • Reposts: once per song, up to 10 a day. Uploads: up to 3 a day.
  • Jams: ones that played for 5+ minutes, up to 3 a day.
  • Plays of your upload: once per listener per song a day.
  • Likes on your reposts: up to 50 a day. Comments on your reposts: once per person per repost, up to 25 people a day.
  • Liking or playing your own posts earns nothing, and neither do your own likes or comments on your reposts.

Questions

What is the Livil music score?

The number under your @name on your Livil profile. It shows how much you listen, share and create on Livil. On Livil, "music score" means this activity score — not sheet music.

How do I increase my Livil music score?

Listen, like, comment, repost songs you love, share songs with friends, host Jams and upload your own music. You also earn when people play your uploads or like and comment on your reposts.

How often does it update?

Once a night. What you do today shows up tomorrow.

Can my music score go down?

Yes — undoing something removes its points: unliking, deleting a comment or a repost, or a song being taken down.

Who can see my music score?

Anyone signed in to Livil, except people you've blocked.
