Delete Your Account

A social music platform built around what you listen to.

Last updated: June 28, 2026

This page explains how to delete your Livil account and the personal data associated with it. You can request deletion at any time, for any reason — no explanation required.

1. Delete From Inside the App

The fastest way to delete your account:

  1. Open Livil and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Tap the settings icon.
  3. Open Account settings.
  4. Select Delete account.
  5. Confirm the action when prompted.

Deletion happens immediately. There is no deactivation step, no waiting period, and no way to cancel or recover the account afterwards. This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

2. Request Deletion by Email

If you can no longer access the app, you can request deletion by email. Send a message from the email address associated with your Livil account to the address below with the subject line “Delete my account”, including your username so we can locate your account.

Livil Support vvk.iitkgp@gmail.com

We will verify your identity and process the request within 30 days.

3. What Data Is Deleted

When your account is deleted, we permanently remove:

  • Your profile — username, display name, bio, and avatar.
  • Your email address and authentication credentials.
  • Your posts and uploaded media (audio and video).
  • Your social activity — likes, follows, comments, and playlists.
  • Your listening history and preferences.
  • Your messages. Messages you sent in group chats are deleted. A one-to-one conversation is deleted in full — for you and for the other person, including the messages they sent in it. Group conversations themselves remain for their other members.

Your username is not released. It is recorded as taken so that nobody else can register it and appear to be you. Only the same email address can claim it again if you make a new account.

4. What May Be Retained

A limited amount of data may be retained for a short period after deletion where required for legal, security, or fraud-prevention purposes — for example, transaction or abuse-prevention records. Such data is kept only as long as necessary and is then permanently deleted.

Content you shared that has been saved or re-shared by other users (where applicable) may persist outside of your account.

5. Retention Period

Account and personal data scheduled for deletion is permanently removed within 30 days of your request, except for the limited categories described in Section 4.
