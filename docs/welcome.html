Early access

You're in.

Livil is a social music platform — a place to listen with people, not beside them. Four short steps, with one short wait in the middle that's Google's doing rather than a mistake on your end.

Use the same Google account for every step. Play checks your testing access against the account you join with, so mixing two accounts is the one thing that reliably breaks this.
  1. 1

    Join the testers group

    This is what authorises your Google account for the test build.

    Join the group Tap Join group at the top of that page. Two things you'll see that look wrong but aren't:

    · “You don't have permission to access this content” under the button — ignore it. That's the group's message board, closed on purpose.
    · A Subscription dropdown — set it to No email. Nobody can post to this group, so it's just a roster; you'll never get mail from it.
  2. 2

    Accept the test invite

    Confirms you as a tester. Do this after step 1, or the page won't recognise you.

    Become a tester Look for Become a tester. Once accepted, it'll say you're a tester and give you a download link.

    If it says “App not available” instead, that's normal. Google takes a few minutes to register you after you join the group — sometimes an hour or two. Nothing went wrong and step 1 does not need doing again. Close this, come back later, and tap the link once more.
  3. 3

    Install Livil

    Android only for now — an iOS build isn't out yet.

    Open in Play Store If the Store says the app isn't available here too, step 2 hasn't gone through yet — same wait, same fix. Once you're through step 2, this link works.
  4. 4

    Create your account

    Open the app and sign up. You'll pick a handle — choose carefully, it's permanent. Confirm the email we send and you're in.

    Check spam if the confirmation doesn't arrive within a minute or two.

Make music yourself? The creator studio runs in this browser right now — upload masters straight from your desktop, no phone needed.

Stuck on any step, just reply to the invite email. — Vamsi
