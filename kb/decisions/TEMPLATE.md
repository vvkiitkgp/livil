## Context

What forced a decision. The constraints that were real at the time, including the ones that no
longer apply — a future reader needs to know *why this was hard*, not just what was chosen.

State facts, not conclusions. If a measurement drove this, give the number.

## Decision

What we chose, stated plainly enough that someone can tell whether a future change violates it.

## Alternatives considered

**The most valuable section.** The rejected option is the one someone proposes again next year
(Constitution P11).

## Consequences

What this makes easy, what it makes hard, and what it forecloses. Include the costs — an ADR
that lists only benefits is advocacy, not a record.

## Dissent

Positions argued and not adopted, recorded rather than erased (P10, P62). If a minority view
later proves right, this is the most valuable line in the document.

*None recorded.* — or the argument, attributed.

## Revisit when

Concrete triggers, not "periodically". What would have to become true for this to be worth
reopening?
