# Content Pillars

> This document defines **what LiViL talks about**.
>
> A strong brand isn't built by posting randomly—it is built by repeatedly reinforcing a small set of ideas.
>
> Every Instagram post, carousel, presentation, reel, thread, or article should support one or more of these pillars.

---

# Philosophy

LiViL is not trying to become another account that constantly promotes its own product.

Instead, LiViL documents the journey of building a thoughtful social music platform.

People should follow because they enjoy the ideas, the craftsmanship, and the progress—not because they're being advertised to.

Every piece of content should satisfy at least one of these goals:

- Teach something.
- Show meaningful progress.
- Inspire creators.
- Start a discussion.
- Demonstrate craftsmanship.
- Celebrate music.

# Content Mix

A healthy month should include a balance of all pillars.

Avoid posting three Product posts in a row.

Rotate naturally.

Example:

Week 1

Product

Engineering

Music

Week 2

Founder

Design

Product

Week 3

Engineering

Community

Product

Week 4

Experiment

Founder

Design

---

# What We Don't Post

Avoid content that exists only to increase engagement.

Do not create:

- Clickbait
- Rage bait
- Trend chasing
- Meme reposts
- Generic motivational quotes
- Empty announcements
- Feature dumps
- AI-generated filler

Every post should have a reason to exist.

---

# The 80/20 Rule

80% of our content should create value.

20% can directly promote LiViL.

Promotion is earned by consistently providing value.

---

# The Litmus Test

Before publishing, ask:

Does this post make someone:

- Learn?
- Think?
- Feel inspired?
- Appreciate good design?
- Appreciate good engineering?
- Love music a little more?

If the answer is "no," rethink the post.

---

# Long-Term Goal

LiViL should become known for documenting the craft of building products for music lovers.

Followers shouldn't just remember our features.

They should remember how we think.
