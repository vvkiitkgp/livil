# LiViL Brand Guide

This document defines the personality, visual identity, communication style, and overall feeling of LiViL.

Every piece of content, design, image, presentation, social media post, landing page, or promotional material should align with this guide.

---

# Brand Mission

LiViL exists to make music social again.

Streaming made music personal.

LiViL makes music collaborative.

Music should create conversations, friendships, communities, and shared experiences—not isolated listening sessions.

---

# Vision

Build the social platform for music.

Not another streaming app.

A place where music connects people.

---

# Brand Personality

LiViL should feel:

• Premium
• Calm
• Confident
• Modern
• Minimal
• Creative
• Human
• Curious
• Engineering-driven
• Music-first

Never loud.

Never flashy.

Never childish.

Never overly corporate.

---

# Brand Values

People over algorithms.

Community over virality.

Quality over quantity.

Design over decoration.

Craft over speed.

Authenticity over marketing.

---

# Emotional Goal

Every interaction with LiViL should make people feel:

"I'm discovering music with people."

instead of

"I'm consuming content."

---

# Audience

Primary

• Music lovers
• Independent artists
• Producers
• Engineers
• Designers
• Startup founders
• Early adopters

Secondary

Anyone who appreciates beautifully designed products.

---

# Tone of Voice

Write like a thoughtful founder.

Not a marketing team.

Sentences should be:

Short.

Clear.

Confident.

Simple.

Never exaggerated.

---

# Avoid

Do not use phrases like:

- Revolutionary
- Game-changing
- Next generation
- Industry-leading
- Ultimate platform
- Best ever
- Disrupting music

Avoid unnecessary emojis.

Avoid hype.

Avoid fake urgency.

Avoid clickbait.

---

# What Success Looks Like

People should describe LiViL as:

"A beautiful product."

"A thoughtful company."

"A startup I'd love to work at."

"These people really care about music."

---

# Design Inspiration

Take inspiration from companies such as:

- Apple
- Linear
- Raycast
- Stripe
- Arc Browser
- Notion
- Figma
- Vercel

Do not copy.

Understand why their communication feels premium.

---

# Public Image

Every post should communicate one of these:

We build carefully.

We care about details.

We love music.

We enjoy solving difficult problems.

We are transparent about the journey.

We ship consistently.

---

# Brand Promise

Every feature should strengthen one simple idea:

Music is better together.
