# Content Playbook

> Every feature, redesign, bug fix, experiment, or engineering challenge can generate multiple pieces of content.

The purpose of this document is to ensure LiViL never runs out of meaningful things to share.

We do not create content for the sake of posting.

We document the journey of building a thoughtful product.

---

# Philosophy

One feature should never become one Instagram post.

One feature should become an entire story.

Every significant improvement can be viewed from multiple perspectives.

Different audiences care about different aspects.

A designer notices spacing.

A developer notices architecture.

A musician notices the experience.

A founder notices the decision making.

Our job is to tell every one of those stories.

---

# Content Extraction Framework

Whenever a new feature is completed, ask:

Why was it built?

What problem existed?

What alternatives were explored?

What engineering challenges appeared?

What design iterations happened?

What surprised us?

What tradeoffs were made?

What did we remove?

What did we learn?

What should happen next?

Every answer is potential content.

# Product Update Pipeline

Every shipped feature should follow this process.

Step 1

Capture screenshots.

Step 2

Record short videos.

Step 3

Save development screenshots.

Step 4

Save early mockups.

Step 5

Write down interesting engineering decisions.

Step 6

Write down mistakes.

Step 7

Collect user feedback.

Step 8

Generate content ideas.

Never rely on memory.

# Content Checklist

Before publishing ask:

Does this teach something?

Does this tell a story?

Does this showcase craftsmanship?

Does this make someone appreciate the work?

Would I enjoy seeing this if it wasn't my company?

If the answer is "no," improve the story.

---

# Long-Term Goal

LiViL's content should become a living documentary.

Years from now, someone should be able to scroll back and watch the company grow from its earliest prototypes into a mature product.

That history is one of our greatest assets.
